FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what customers usually ask before booking a survey. Can't see what you need? Get in touch.

Do you install air conditioning in homes and businesses?

Yes — we install and maintain both domestic and commercial air conditioning across Chelmsford and mid Essex. We don't carry out refrigeration work.

How long does an installation take?

A single-room, wall-mounted split usually takes one day, and we tidy up before we leave. Multi-room or commercial jobs take longer — we'll confirm a schedule after the survey.

Will I need planning permission?

Most domestic outdoor units fall under permitted development, as long as they're positioned correctly relative to the boundary, roofline and road, and stay within current noise limits. Listed buildings, conservation areas and flats generally need explicit planning consent — we check this as a standard part of every survey.

How much does an air conditioning installation cost?

It depends on the system and the job, but as a guide: a single-room split typically runs £1,500–£2,800, a multi-split covering two to four rooms £3,000–£6,500, and ducted or whole-house systems £5,000–£15,000+. We'll give you an exact fixed price after a free survey.

Will it cost a lot to run?

A typical 2.5kW inverter split costs around 18–21p an hour to run at current electricity prices — most homes only run it on the hottest days, so ongoing cost is usually modest.

Are you F-Gas registered?

Yes. F-Gas company certification is a legal requirement to install, service or decommission any system containing F-Gas refrigerant, and our engineers hold the relevant City & Guilds qualifications.

Do you offer maintenance and servicing?

Yes — annual servicing keeps your system running efficiently and keeps it under warranty, typically from £80 a year. We also handle repairs and call-outs.

What warranty do you offer?

Every installation comes with a five-year warranty.

What areas do you cover?

Chelmsford and mid Essex. If you're just outside that area, get in touch and we'll let you know if we can help.
