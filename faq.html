FAQs

Questions we get asked

If yours isn't here, get in touch — we'd rather answer it properly than have you guess.

How long does an installation take?
A single-room system is usually one day. A multi-split running several rooms takes two to three. We'll tell you exactly when we quote, and we don't leave a job half finished overnight.
How much mess is involved?
We core-drill one hole per indoor unit, roughly 65mm, and we lay dust sheets before anything starts. We vacuum up and take all packaging away with us.
Will it be noisy?
Modern indoor units run at about 19–25 decibels on low — quieter than a fridge. The outdoor unit is louder, so where we site it matters; we'll talk through the options at the survey.
Do I need planning permission?
For most houses, no — a domestic outdoor unit falls under permitted development if it meets size and siting rules. Flats, listed buildings and conservation areas are different, and we'll flag it if yours is.
Can air conditioning heat the house too?
Yes. Every system we fit is a heat pump running in reverse, so it heats as efficiently as it cools. Plenty of customers use theirs more in spring and autumn than in summer.
What does it cost to run?
It depends on the unit, the room and how you use it, but a modern inverter system is far cheaper to run than the old fixed-speed kit. We'll give you an honest estimate for your rooms rather than a headline figure.
Do you service systems you didn't install?
Yes, as long as it's a make we can get parts for. We'll tell you at the first visit if we think a system is beyond sensible repair.
What warranty do I get?
Five years on our workmanship, plus the manufacturer's warranty on the equipment — usually five to seven years, provided it's serviced annually. We send you a reminder when it's due.

Still not sure what you need? A survey is free and takes about 45 minutes.
