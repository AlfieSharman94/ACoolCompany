Why pair solar with air conditioning

A typical 2.5kW inverter split costs around 18–21p an hour to run at current electricity prices, mostly on the hottest, sunniest days — exactly when solar panels generate the most. Installing both together means more of that running cost is covered by your own generation rather than the grid.
